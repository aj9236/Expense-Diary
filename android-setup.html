Set up your Expense Diary

Your records will be kept in your own Google account. Only you can see them. This takes about ten minutes, once. Use Chrome on this phone, signed in with your Google account.

1

Open the service and make your copy

Open in Chrome

If the page looks cramped or asks for a computer, tap Chrome's ⋮ menu and tick Desktop site.

Tap the Make a copy button: two overlapping pages at the very top right of the “Project Details” page. On a phone it is often just off the screen. Switch on Desktop site (Chrome ⋮ menu), then pinch to zoom out or turn the phone sideways until you see it. Your own copy then opens.

You must be signed in to your Google account in Chrome; if the page asks you to sign in, do that first.

2

Run setup and allow access

In your copy, make sure setup is chosen next to Run, then tap Run.

Tap Review permissions and choose your account. Google warns that the app isn't verified, because it is your own private copy: tap Advanced, then Go to … (unsafe), tick Select all and tap Continue.

Wait until the log shows "Execution completed". A Google Sheet named "Expense Diary" is created in your Drive.

3

Publish it for your phone

Tap Deploy, then New deployment. Tap the gear next to "Select type" and choose Web app.

Set Who has access to Anyone and tap Deploy. If Google asks again, allow access as in step 2.

Copy the Web app URL that appears.

4

Connect the app

Paste the address into Chrome's address bar, add ?connect at the very end, and open it.

Tap Connect the Android app and choose Expense Diary if asked. The app opens, already connected. If the button does nothing, copy the setup link shown on that page, open Expense Diary → Settings, paste it into Web app URL and tap Save and connect.

5

Add bank SMS in two taps

In your Messages app, press and hold a bank SMS, tap Share and choose Expense Diary. The transaction appears on the Confirm page; tick it and tap Add to diary.

Cash spends and receipts are added with the + button.

6

Your records and backup

Everything is saved in the "Expense Diary" Google Sheet in your Google account. In the app, Settings has buttons to open the Sheet and to download an Excel backup.

Nothing is added to your diary until you tick it and tap Add to diary. Your records stay in your own Google account.
